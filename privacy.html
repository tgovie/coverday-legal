Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

Coverday helps you see whether your bills are covered until your next payday. This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens to it. We wrote it in plain language on purpose.

The short version

  • Everything you enter (bills, paydays, balances, spending and settings) is stored only on your device. On the web version at coverday.expo.app, it’s stored only in your browser.
  • We don’t have accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • When you import a statement, the names of places Coverday doesn’t recognize (for example “TRH MONTREAL”) may be sent to our server so an AI service can suggest a category. Amounts, dates and account numbers are never sent.
  • Coverday never asks for your bank username or password.
  • You can erase everything at any time in Settings → Erase all data, or by deleting the app.

Information you enter

You may enter bill names, amounts, due dates, categories, notes, income sources, paydays, an account balance and card or bank transactions you import from a statement. This information is saved in the app’s private storage on your device, or in your browser’s storage if you use the web version. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, except as described under “Sorting unfamiliar places” below.

Sorting unfamiliar places

When you import a statement, Coverday sorts charges on your device first. For places it doesn’t recognize, it may send only the merchant description from your statement (such as “SQ *CELEBRITIES VANCOUVER”) to the Coverday server, which asks Anthropic’s Claude AI service what kind of business it is. Amounts, dates, account numbers and your file itself are not sent. We don’t store these descriptions. Anthropic processes them under its commercial terms and does not use them to train its models. If you’re offline, this step is skipped and those charges stay in Other.

Information we collect automatically

None. Coverday does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising identifiers or third-party tracking software.

Apple and Google may give developers anonymous, aggregated crash and usage statistics if you’ve chosen to share them with developers in your device settings. That sharing is controlled by your device, not by Coverday.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your information. Apart from the merchant descriptions described above, it never leaves your device.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your device until you erase it in Settings or delete the app. On the web version, it stays in your browser until you erase it in Settings or clear your browser’s site data. Device backups (such as iCloud or Google backups) may include app data, according to your device’s backup settings.

Children

Coverday is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If a future version adds features that send data off your device, such as accounts, sync or bank connections, we will update this policy before releasing that version and explain what changes. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
