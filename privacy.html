Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Coverday helps you see whether your bills are covered until your next payday. This policy explains what information the app handles and what happens to it. We wrote it in plain language on purpose.

The short version

  • Everything you enter (bills, paydays, balances and settings) is stored only on your device.
  • We don’t have accounts, servers that receive your data, analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • Coverday never asks for your bank username or password.
  • You can erase everything at any time in Settings → Erase all data, or by deleting the app.

Information you enter

You may enter bill names, amounts, due dates, categories, notes, income sources, paydays and an account balance. This information is saved in the app’s private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Information we collect automatically

None. Coverday does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising identifiers or third-party tracking software.

Apple and Google may give developers anonymous, aggregated crash and usage statistics if you’ve chosen to share them with developers in your device settings. That sharing is controlled by your device, not by Coverday.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your information. We cannot see it, because it never leaves your device.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your device until you erase it in Settings or delete the app. Device backups (such as iCloud or Google backups) may include app data, according to your device’s backup settings.

Children

Coverday is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If a future version adds features that send data off your device, such as accounts, sync or bank connections, we will update this policy before releasing that version and explain what changes. The date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
